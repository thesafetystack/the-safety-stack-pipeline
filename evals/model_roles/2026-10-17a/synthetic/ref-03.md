A week of asking a chatbot about my taxes

In early 2024 I spent a week asking ChatGPT the tax questions I would normally take to an accountant: deductions for a home office, how to report freelance income, and what counts as a business expense. I asked about fifty questions in all.

It got most of the simple ones right, and its explanations were clearer than the official guidance. It was much less reliable on questions that depended on the exact year's rules: twice it confidently quoted a limit that had changed. It also never asked which country I was in until I mentioned it.

My conclusion is that ChatGPT is a good first stop for understanding a tax question, but not something I would file a return on without checking.
